# Online Furniture 🛋️
**Premium Craftsmanship. Direct to Your Home.**

Online Furniture is a specialized e-commerce platform for **Bhola Sofa Centre**, based in Hazaribagh. We provide high-quality, custom-made furniture with a focus on luxury L-shape sofas and premium upholstery materials.

## 🚀 Features
- **Digital Showroom:** High-resolution gallery featuring our exclusive collection (Royal Curve, Premium Headrest, and Butterfly Handle designs).
- **Smart Shopping Cart:** Customers can select multiple items to build their dream living room set.
- **WhatsApp Order Integration:** Direct-to-owner order notifications. No middleman, no delays.
- **Instant Billing:** Automatic calculation of totals and GST, sent directly to the customer and owner via WhatsApp.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Mobile-first responsive design).
- **Logic:** Vanilla JavaScript (Cart management & Billing calculations).
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages.
- **Communication:** WhatsApp Business API integration.

## 📦 How to Order
1. Browse the **Exclusive Collection**.
2. Click **"Add to Order"** for the items you desire.
3. Review your **Order List** at the bottom of the page.
4. Enter your name and click **"Place Order"**.
5. Your WhatsApp will open with a pre-filled bill summary to send to our team.
